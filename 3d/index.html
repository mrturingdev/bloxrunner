Run. Jump. Survive.

by Pranav Maharjan

Bloxrunner

Tap, click or press Space to jump. Hold for a higher jump, and tap again in mid-air to double jump. Skins with a power get a button (or press E) that recharges in 90 seconds. Duck with the down arrow. Grab ⚡ to smash through obstacles and ⏳ to slow time.
